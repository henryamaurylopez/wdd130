Dominican Republic

The Dominican Republic is a country in the Americas situated in the Caribbean, located in the central part of the Antilles; it occupies the central and eastern portions of the island of Hispaniola. Its capital and most populous city is Santo Domingo. It is bordered to the north by the Atlantic Ocean, to the east by the Mona Passage (which separates it from Puerto Rico), to the south by the Caribbean Sea, and to the west by Haiti, the other country located on Hispaniola. With an area of ​​48,448 km² and a population exceeding 11.4 million inhabitants as of September 2024, it is the second-largest Caribbean island nation—after Cuba—and the third most populous, after Haiti and Cuba. It is part of the Spanish-speaking Caribbean.

Christopher Columbus landed in 1492 in the country's territory—inhabited by the Taíno people since the 7th century—making it the site of the first permanent European settlement in the Americas.
